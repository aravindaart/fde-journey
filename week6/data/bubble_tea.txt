Bubble Tea Lab is Singapore's most dinosaur-obsessed bubble tea brand, founded in 2021
by cousins who believed that great tea and prehistoric chaos belong together. Our flagship
store is located at 12 Jurong West Street 52, #01-03, Singapore 649268. A second outlet
opened in Clementi Mall in 2023, and a third is planned for Tampines in late 2026.

We are open Monday to Friday from 10am to 10pm. On weekends and public holidays we open
earlier at 9am and close at 11pm. Last order is taken 15 minutes before closing time.
During the June and December school holidays we extend weekday hours to 11pm.

Our kitchen operates a strict allergen policy. The preparation area is fully nut-free
except for two drinks: the Galactic Pistachio Cheese Foam Supreme and the Fossil Praline
Crunch Shake. Customers with nut allergies must inform staff before ordering. All other
drinks and snacks are prepared in a dedicated nut-free zone with separate utensils.

SIGNATURE DRINKS

The Taro Meteor Shake is made with real Taiwanese taro root, slow-cooked for four hours
before blending. It is available in regular (M) and large (L) sizes at $6.90 and $8.20
respectively. Customisation options include sugar level (0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 100%) and
ice level (no ice, less ice, regular ice).

The Mango Comet Splash uses fresh Alphonso mangoes imported from India during peak season
(April to June) and switching to frozen Taiwanese mango outside season. Regular is $7.20
and large is $8.50. This drink is not available with sugar reduction below 50% because
the mango base is naturally sweetened.

The Brown Sugar Fossil Latte is our bestselling drink. It uses hand-burnt Okinawa brown
sugar syrup layered over fresh milk and tiger pearls. Regular is $6.50, large is $7.80.
The syrup is made in-house every morning at 9am. After 8pm any remaining syrup is used
up and the drink may not be available. Staff will advise at point of order.

PREMIUM CREATIONS

The Galactic Pistachio Cheese Foam Supreme with Crystal Pearls and Toasted Coconut Bits
is our most complex drink. It requires 8 minutes to prepare and cannot be rushed. It is
priced at $12.80 for a single size only — no large option exists for this drink. It is
only available after 5pm on weekdays and from opening time on weekends. Customers who
order this drink before 5pm on a weekday will be advised to return later or choose
an alternative.

The Fossil Praline Crunch Shake combines house-made praline with Hokkaido milk and
crushed graham crackers. It is priced at $11.50 and contains nuts. Available all day.
Large size is available at $13.20. This drink sells out frequently on weekends —
customers are advised to arrive before 2pm to avoid disappointment.

The Lychee Nebula Tea uses lychee imported from Thailand, served over hand-cracked ice
with butterfly pea flower cold brew for a colour-changing effect. Priced at $5.80 regular
and $7.10 large. The colour change requires no ice modification — ice level cannot be
changed for this drink.

The Matcha Volcano Crush uses ceremonial-grade Uji matcha from Kyoto, Japan. It is
available hot or iced. Hot is $6.70 in one size only. Iced is $6.70 regular and $8.00
large. Matcha sourcing may change outside of the Japanese harvest season (March to May).

SNACKS

Dino Egg Mochi is made fresh every morning in-store. It comes in three flavours: original
(plain mochi with red bean filling), chocolate lava, and seasonal. The seasonal flavour
changes monthly. All three are priced at $4.20 per portion of three pieces. Mochi sells
out by early afternoon on most days — pre-ordering via the app is recommended.

Jurassic Fries come in regular ($5.10) and large ($7.50). They are seasoned with Bubble
house spice blend and served with two dipping sauces: garlic aioli and sriracha mayo.
A loaded version with cheese sauce is available for an additional $1.50 on top of either
size. Jurassic Fries are prepared in the same fryer as the mochi — both are gluten-free.

LOYALTY AND MEMBERSHIP

Bubble membership is free. Customers can register in 2 minutes via the Bubble app,
available on iOS and Android. Walk-in registration at the counter is also available but
takes longer during peak hours.

Members earn 1 reward point for every $1 spent. Points are rounded down to the nearest
dollar — a $6.90 purchase earns 6 points. Points expire 12 months after the date they
were earned, not 12 months after the last transaction. Customers frequently misunderstand
this — each point has its own expiry, not the account.

Every 100 points can be redeemed for one free drink of any size up to large. Premium
creations (Galactic Pistachio and Fossil Praline) are excluded from redemption. Snacks
cannot be redeemed with points at this time but this policy is under review.

Members also receive a free drink on their birthday month. The birthday drink is capped
at $7.00 in value. If the chosen drink exceeds $7.00, the member pays the difference.
Birthday drinks cannot be combined with point redemption in the same transaction.

ORDERING AND OPERATIONS

All orders can be placed at the counter or via the Bubble app. App orders receive a
5-minute priority queue advantage during peak hours (12pm to 2pm and 6pm to 8pm).
Walk-in orders during peak hours may wait up to 20 minutes for premium creations.

Bubble does not currently offer island-wide delivery. A pilot delivery programme
covering Jurong West, Clementi, and Bukit Batok is running until August 2026 via the
app only. Delivery orders have a minimum spend of $15 and a flat delivery fee of $3.

Group orders of 10 or more drinks qualify for a 10% discount. Group orders must be
placed at least 2 hours in advance via the app or by calling the store directly.
The discount does not apply to premium creations or during school holiday extended hours.
